### Slide 2: Thermal Clamps & Indium Foil
**Visual:** `slide_2.png`

> **Technical Narration (Spoken Audio):**
> "Modification 1 covers thermal dissipation. Installing a CNC solid clamp bar provides uniform pressure across the transistor flange, while replacing silicone grease with 0.1 millimeter Indium foil eliminates thermal pump-out and lowers operating temperatures by up to 12 degrees Celsius."

---

### Slide 3: 16 dB Input Attenuator Upgrade
**Visual:** `slide_3.png`

> **Technical Narration (Spoken Audio):**
> "Modification 2 is the 16 dB input attenuator. Modern 100-watt transceivers can produce brief power spikes before internal ALC loops settle. A 16 dB pad allows your transceiver to drive at 50 watts in its cleanest linear operating range while protecting the amplifier input."

---

### Slide 4: LPF ATC Capacitors & Coupler Shielding
**Visual:** `slide_4.png`

> **Technical Narration (Spoken Audio):**
> "Modifications 3 and 4 harden the low-pass filter and SWR coupler. Upgrading to high-Q ATC porcelain capacitors handles high circulating RF currents on 10 and 12 meters, while copper foil shielding over the directional coupler prevents stray RF pickup on 6 meters."

---

### Slide 5: Ferrite Decoupling & RFI Hardening
**Visual:** `slide_5.png`

> **Technical Narration (Spoken Audio):**
> "Modification 5 addresses station RFI hardening. Installing Mix 31 and Mix 43 ferrite beads on HDMI and sensor ribbon cables prevents common-mode RF currents from locking up the touchscreen or disrupting telemetry during legal-limit transmission."
