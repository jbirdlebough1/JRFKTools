01 The MicroSD Fleet Strategy

The JRFK architecture maintains a multi-card defense fleet so that no single hardware degradation can ever take your station offline:

Card ID Physical Location Condition Role in Emergency Recovery
PROD-64 Internal RPi Slot Active 64-Bit Production Current operational system running the amplifier. Subject to wear or sudden failure.
BACKUP-AUTO External USB 2.0 Slot Weekly Clone Target Contains full raw binary backup generated automatically every Sunday at 04:00 GMT. Primary swap replacement!

02 Rapid Field Hot-Swap Procedure (Under 3 Minutes)

STEP 1

Immediate Shutdown & AC Isolation

Switch the front/rear AC power switch to OFF. Disconnect the main AC power cord from the wall outlet. Wait 30 seconds for the internal Eltek Flatpack2 high-capacity filter capacitors to bleed down safely.

STEP 2

Extract Degraded MicroSD

Carefully press and release the degraded MicroSD card (PROD-64) located in the push-pull card slot on the Raspberry Pi. Remove the card and set it aside for post-incident diagnostic examination on your PC.

STEP 3

Insert Backup SD Card

Retrieve either your BACKUP-AUTO card (from the amplifier's USB reader) or your pre-provisioned FIELD-64 spare card. Firmly insert it into the Raspberry Pi internal slot until it clicks securely into place.

STEP 4

Power On & Initial Boot Verification

Reconnect the AC power cord and switch the AC power switch to ON. The Raspberry Pi will immediately boot:

  • Display Handshake: The 1024x600 LCD touchscreen illuminates with the RF-KIT boot logo (X11 desktop is completely suppressed).
  • Controller Connection: The RF-KIT GUI links to /dev/ttyUSB_RFK, displaying DC Bus Voltage (~53.5V) and PA Temperature.
  • Station Network Online: The amplifier connects to Wi-Fi/Ethernet; web console responds at http://<amplifier-ip>:8088/.

Your amplifier is now 100% operational and ready to transmit.

03 Weekly Automated Backups & Autopatching Overview

Your JRFK 64-bit installation maintains an active system cron (/etc/cron.d/jrfk_weekly_backup) that guarantees your backup card is never out of date:

  • Schedule: Every Sunday morning at 04:00 GMT.
  • Binary Backup: Compresses the entire production SD card into a timestamped image in /home/pi/backups/.
  • Automated OS Autopatching: Immediately following backup creation, the cron executes sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade -y. This ensures your Debian Trixie 64-bit kernel, security packages, and network drivers remain continually updated with zero manual overhead.

04 On-Demand Web Backup & Clone Utility

To create a fresh manual backup at any time before major shack changes or firmware upgrades:

  1. Open your browser to http://<amplifier-ip>:8088/backup.
  2. Click ⚡ Run Full SD Backup Now.
  3. Monitor live streaming progress in the web log. When complete, the backup image is validated and saved.
