01 Historical Provenance & Controller Architectural Parity

The alignment principles, detector calibration formulas, and potentiometer adjustment procedures documented in this Cookbook originate from John Birdlebough's (K7BIT) seminal work on the RF-KIT B26-RF2K+ linear amplifier. Over successive production generations, two primary microcontroller backplane variants have been deployed:

  • First-Generation Backplane: Based on the standard Arduino Mega 2560 R3 daughtercard plugged into the motherboard.
  • Updated Production Controller: Features an integrated proprietary surface-mount microcontroller backplane deployed in the RF2K-S and modern RF2K+ amplifiers.

While RF-KIT has declined to release proprietary schematics for the updated controller board, rigorous bus analysis and reverse-engineering confirm that operational circuit parity is 100% maintained. The analog sensing circuitry, the 9 potentiometer calibration trimmers (R1, R2, R14, R15, R17, R19, R20, R21, SPARE), the relay drivers, and the binary SimpleRPC protocol are identical across all hardware revisions. Every calibration step in this cookbook applies universally to both controller variants.

02 Hardware Architecture & Test Bench Configuration

Subsystem Architecture

  • Main DC Power Supply: High-efficiency Heiwoo 53.5V Switching Power Supply (53.5 VDC @ ~55 A / 3000 W capacity). The power supply is managed via discrete hardware AC line switching and standby/operate inhibit control lines. Note: CAN bus communication is NOT used for power supply control in production units.
  • RF Power Deck: Dual Ampleon BLF189 / BLF189XR 50V LDMOS transistors configured in a high-efficiency push-pull topology on a copper heat-spreader.
  • Low Pass Filter & ATU: Toroidal low-pass filters (160m to 6m) and an integrated 8-step inductor / 8-step capacitor automatic antenna tuner (ATU).

The PA Bypass Test Harness (Safety Isolation)

To safely calibrate the low-level logic, frequency counter, and SWR detectors without risking damage to the Ampleon BLF189 LDMOS transistors:

  1. Disconnect the SMA cable from the RF2K5 PA pallet RF input.
  2. Leave PA output solder pads disconnected from the 11.5-inch PTFE coax.
  3. Connect the transceiver drive directly through the LPF and SWR bridge via the bypass SMA link. Transceiver drive now passes safely to the wattmeter and load without energizing the PA pallet!

04 The 17-Step Precision Bench Calibration Procedure

Perform the 17 steps in exact chronological order to ensure safe and accurate alignment:

Phase 1: Initial Safety Presets & DC Rail Alignment

STEP 1 Temporarily Disable Input Overdrive Trip (R1)

Turn potentiometer R1 5 full turns counter-clockwise (CCW). This prevents premature comparator tripping during initial drive calibration.

STEP 2 Temporarily Disable LPF Difference Trip (R17)

Turn potentiometer R17 5 full turns counter-clockwise (CCW) to prevent differential trip locks while measuring low-pass filters.

STEP 3 Preset SPARE Potentiometer

Locate the SPARE potentiometer positioned below R21 and above R17. Turn 5 full turns counter-clockwise (CCW).

STEP 4 Calibrate 53.5V DC Voltage Display (R2)

Energize the amplifier on AC mains with PA bias jumpers set to Bypass. Connect your calibrated DMM across the Heiwoo DC power supply output bus studs (target: 53.5 VDC). Observe the front-panel touch display voltage. Adjust multiturn potentiometer R2 until the display matches the DMM within ±0.1 VDC.

STEP 5 Verify PA Pallet Bias Inhibit Jumpers

Confirm that both 2-pin bias jumpers on the RF2K5 PA pallet are set to the REAR position (Disabled/Bypass). This guarantees gate bias cannot be energized while initial RF switching tests are executed.

STEP 6 RF Coaxial Harness Continuity & Port Verification

Connect transceiver at 14.175 MHz CW (5W drive) through the PA bypass harness into the 50Ω dummy load at ANT 1. Key transceiver and verify with in-line wattmeter that 5W passes through with < 1.1:1 SWR, proving all relays and coaxial connections are sound.

STEP 7 Frequency Counter Prescaler Calibration (C18 / R_FREQ)

Navigate on the GUI to Menu -> Hardware -> Frequency Detector. Set transceiver to 28.500 MHz CW at 10W drive. Key transceiver and observe display. Adjust ceramic trimmer capacitor C18 on the backplane until the counter reads exactly 28,500 kHz. Test across 1.8 MHz, 7.0 MHz, 14.0 MHz, and 50.0 MHz to confirm clean counting.

STEP 8 Low Pass Filter (LPF) Auto-Selection Verification

Verify that switching bands on the transceiver instantly energizes the matching toroidal low-pass filter relay bank within 15 ms in both UNIVERSAL RF-sensing mode and CAT interface mode.

Phase 2: SWR Bridge Nulling & Power Calibration

STEP 9 SWR Bridge Balance & Reflected Detector Nulling

Connect certified 50Ω dummy load to ANT 1. Temporarily reverse TRX and ANT 1 coaxial cables (transceiver connected to ANT 1, dummy load to TRX input). Set transceiver to 50.0 MHz CW at 20W drive. Key transceiver and adjust the ceramic coupling trimmer on the SWR Bridge board using a non-inductive plastic tool for absolute minimum voltage reading at the Reflected test pin (< 5 mV DC null). Restore cables to normal orientation.

STEP 10 Calibrate Forward (R19) and Reflected (R20) Power Displays

Forward Power: Place amplifier in STANDBY. Set transceiver to 14.175 MHz CW at 50W into calibrated external wattmeter. Key transceiver; adjust potentiometer R19 until the front-panel forward power bar reads exactly 50W.
Reflected Power & SWR: Attach coaxial T-connector with two 50Ω dummy loads in parallel (25Ω load = 2.0:1 SWR). Key transceiver at 50W CW; adjust potentiometer R20 until the SWR display reads exactly 2.0:1 SWR. Remove parallel load.

STEP 11 Calibrate Antenna Tuner Pre-Amplifier Gain (R15)

Attach the 25Ω load (2.0:1 SWR). On the GUI, navigate to Menu -> Debug and locate analog reading A1 PowerTunerGain. Set transceiver to 10W CW at 14.175 MHz. Key transceiver and adjust potentiometer R15 until the A1 reading displays exactly 600 ADC counts.

Phase 3: LDMOS Quiescent Current (Idq) Calibration

STEP 12 Restore PA Pallet RF Connections

Power down the amplifier and wait 30 seconds. Disconnect the temporary PA bypass harness. Reconnect the SMA input cable to the RF2K5 pallet RF input. Solder the PA output pads to the 11.5-inch PTFE coaxial cable leading to the Relay Board.

STEP 13 Connect In-Line DMM for Drain Current Measurement

Insert your calibrated DMM (switched to 10A DC current mode) in series with the 53.5V DC positive supply lead feeding the RF2K5 PA pallet.

STEP 14 Enable PA Gate Bias Jumpers & Adjust Idle Current

Move both 2-pin bias jumpers on the RF2K5 pallet to the FORWARD position (Enabled). Power on amplifier in OPERATE mode (no RF drive applied). Key the PTT line. Adjust the gate bias trimmers on the PA pallet until the DMM reads the specified idle current for the Ampleon BLF189 / BLF189XR transistors (typically 1.8A to 2.2A per device, total ~3.6A to 4.4A).

STEP 15 Calibrate Quiescent Current Display (R21)

While PTT is keyed at idle bias, observe the current readout on the front-panel touchscreen. Adjust potentiometer R21 until the displayed current matches the physical DMM reading within ±0.05A.

Phase 4: Hardware Protection Comparators

STEP 16 Calibrate Input Overdrive Trip Threshold (R1)

Set transceiver to 28W CW carrier at 14.175 MHz. Slowly turn potentiometer R1 clockwise (CW) while keying until the amplifier hardware instantly trips on "INPUT OVERDRIVE" fault at exactly 28W drive.

STEP 17 Calibrate LPF Forward Power Difference Trip (R17)

Set transceiver to 50W CW drive. In the Debug menu, monitor analog reading A2 LPF_FWD_DIFF. Turn potentiometer R17 clockwise until the reading reaches 93 ADC counts.

05 The 10-Part Comprehensive Characterization Plan

Following calibration, perform this exhaustive 10-part verification suite to validate station readiness:

Test Test Parameter Methodology & Conditions Pass / Fail Criteria
T1 Cold DC Isolation Audit Measure resistance from 53.5V bus to ground (unpowered). R > 100 kΩ after filter cap charge; no shorts.
T2 Low-Voltage Rail Accuracy DMM verification of +12V, +5V, and +3.3V backplane rails. All rails within ±2.5% of nominal voltage.
T3 SWR Bridge Detector Null 50W CW into certified 50Ω load at 50 MHz. Reflected detector voltage < 5 mV DC.
T4 LPF Return Loss Verification Network analyzer / transceiver SWR through all 7 filters. Return loss > 20 dB (SWR < 1.2:1) across passband.
T5 Antenna Matrix Isolation Measure RF isolation between unenergized coaxial ports. Isolation > 55 dB from 1.8 MHz to 54 MHz.
T6 ATU Component Step Audit Verify all 8 L-steps and 8 C-steps latch relay contacts. Inductance 0.05µH to 6.4µH; Capacitance 10pF to 2600pF.
T7 Bias Thermal Stability Key idle bias for 10 minutes; monitor current drift. Idq drift < ±5% over 20°C to 50°C heatsink range.
T8 Harmonic Suppression Spectrum analyzer audit of 2nd and 3rd harmonics at 1500W. All harmonics > 60 dB below fundamental carrier.
T9 Full-Power Saturation & Sag 1500W CW key-down for 30s into high-power load. 53.5V rail sag < 1.5 VDC; RF gain > 14.5 dB.
T10 Protection State Machine Latency Assert high SWR (> 3.0:1) and measure fault trip time. Hardware fault trip latency < 15 ms; PA unkeyed.
